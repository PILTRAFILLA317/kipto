# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who take screenshots as lightweight memory and action capture. They use
Kipto in short, frequent sessions to understand what a screenshot represents,
what still needs attention, and what can be safely archived.

## Product Purpose

Kipto turns screenshots and their metadata into one durable `SavedItem`. Its
primary job is to provide a calm, offline-first inbox for pending intent while
remaining useful when the original image is unavailable locally.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, and dependable. Kipto should feel focused and contemporary without
making routine inbox work theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic gallery grids, decorative dashboard metrics, oversized rounded
cards, noisy gradients, glass effects, gamification, and interactions that imply
cloud, AI, or external integrations exist before they do.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the pending human action, not the screenshot file.
2. Make state and consequence visible before an operation is performed.
3. Prefer familiar platform controls and predictable navigation.
4. Keep offline and unavailable-image states first-class, not exceptional.
5. Use progressive detail so the inbox remains fast to scan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use Material accessibility defaults, readable contrast, scalable text, semantic
labels, minimum practical touch targets, system light/dark modes, and no motion
that is required to understand state.
